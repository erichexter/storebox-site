Fifteen black bins on the shelf. Which one has the Christmas lights?

If you're the kind of person who buys totes by the six-pack and builds a 2×4 rack to hold them, you already know the catch: opaque bins you can't see into, stacked high and heavy, so finding one thing means pulling down and opening five. StoreBox is built for exactly that wall.

How it works

Catalog once. Never dig again.

Three steps, then your whole rack is searchable from your pocket.

1

Label every tote

Each bin gets a code like G-001 and a QR label you print and stick on the front. Scan it later to see inside — before you pull it down.

2

Fill it fast with Blitz

Blitz mode walks you down the rack, logging one tote after another. Snap, list what's inside, next bin. A whole wall in one sitting.

3

Find anything in seconds

Search every bin at once — "extension cords" — and StoreBox tells you the exact tote and which shelf it's on. Done.
