Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything you add lives in a database on your own device. StoreBox works without an account, and we never receive a copy of your inventory. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do the QR labels work?

Each container gets its own code. Print or stick a label on the box, then scan it in the app to jump straight to that container’s contents.

What is the AI photo scan?

It’s an optional feature: take a photo of an open box and StoreBox can suggest the items inside so you don’t have to type each one. It only runs when you ask it to.

Will I lose my data if I get a new phone?

Because your data is stored on the device, use your device’s standard backup and restore to move it to a new phone. Uninstalling the app removes its data, so keep a backup before you do.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any container, item, or photo inside the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.
